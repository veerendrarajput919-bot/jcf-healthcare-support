How JCF Healthcare Aid Works

A transparent 4-stage lifecycle from application submission to doorstep medicine delivery and surgery grants.

📝

Submit Request

Fill our simple patient form with your illness details, needed aid (medicines, surgery, consult), and upload your doctor prescription.

About JCF Healthcare Support

Bridging Medical Inequality With Care & Integrity

Founded with the belief that quality healthcare is a fundamental human right, JCF Healthcare Support works on the frontline to prevent treatable illnesses from becoming fatal due to lack of financial resources.

We operate in strict coordination with certified volunteer clinicians, municipal healthcare workers, and accredited hospitals to deliver medicines, fund emergency pediatric surgeries, and organize free diagnostics.

🎯 100% Transparency

Every rupee and medicine unit is tracked and verified directly with patient case files.

🩺 Board of Specialists

Over 80 senior doctors review critical surgery applications within hours.

⚖️ 80G & 12A Certified

Registered non-profit foundation with statutory compliance and open audit reports.

⚡ Rapid Response

Emergency cases processed within 2-4 hours through dedicated local emergency cells.
